Your ascendancy is the biggest power spike of a build: most guides on this site list their ascendancy notables in the order you should take them. You unlock the class and its points by completing Trials of Ascendancy, two points at a time.

> **The short version.** Ascend in Act 2 with the Trial of the Sekhemas, take points 3 and 4 in Act 3 with the Trial of Chaos, then finish points 5 to 8 in the endgame with higher-level trial keys.

## The Route to All 8 Points

| Points | Trial | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-2 (ascend) | Trial of the Sekhemas, Act 2 | Balbala's Barya, from the campaign quest |
| 3-4 | Trial of Chaos, Act 3 | Chimeral Inscribed Ultimatum, from the boss Xyclucian, the Chimera |
| 5-6 | Sekhemas with 3 floors, **or** Chaos with 10 rounds | Djinn Barya level 60+, **or** Inscribed Ultimatum level 75+ |
| 7-8 | Sekhemas with 4 floors and its pinnacle boss, **or** Chaos with 10 rounds and the locked boss | Djinn Barya level 75+, **or** Inscribed Ultimatum level 75+ with all three Fate fragments |

The endgame keys drop in maps (and level 60+ Baryas can already drop near the end of the campaign). You can also buy them from other players.

> **The key's level sets how far it goes.** A Djinn Barya has 1 floor up to level 44, 2 floors at 45-59, 3 floors at 60-74 and 4 floors at 75+. An Inscribed Ultimatum has 4 rounds up to level 59, 7 rounds at 60-74 and 10 rounds at 75+. Hover the key before you use or buy it: it shows its number of trials, and says so when completing it will not grant more Ascendancy points.

> **Should you ascend in Act 2?** Almost always. Waiting for a later trial means playing without your ascendancy for a long time. If your build struggles, gain a few levels and better gear first, but still do it in Act 2.

## Trial of the Sekhemas

### Finding it

During the Act 2 quest that takes you through the Traitor's Passage, look for a sealed door covered in glyphs. Opening it leads to the fight that gives you Balbala's Barya, the key to the trial. If you cannot find the door, look for the lore pillar next to it on your minimap.

### Honour: the extra health bar

Inside the trial you have **Honour**, a separate bar based on your life and energy shield. Every time you take damage, you also lose Honour, and you fail the trial when it reaches zero, even if you are alive. Flasks, regeneration and leech do **not** restore it: only some rooms, boons and relics do.

So the trial rewards careful play over speed:

- Monsters have slow, telegraphed attacks and every trap can be avoided. Take your time.
- Check the map before picking the next room: it shows the challenge type, the reward and any affliction.
- When your Honour is low, skip risky rooms, even if they promise good loot.
- If you fail, you can try again as many times as you need.

### Relics

Relics are slotted for the trial and make higher floors far easier. In order of priority:

1. **Honour resistance**, up to 75%. It applies after your normal resistances: a 1,000 fire damage hit at 75% fire resistance becomes 250, and only about 63 Honour damage with 75% Honour resistance.
2. **Maximum Honour.**
3. Then movement speed, damage against bosses and the rest.

## Trial of Chaos

### Finding it

The entrance is in Act 3's Chimeral Wetlands, along an outer edge of the zone, up a narrow passage with stone stairs. You need a Chimeral Inscribed Ultimatum, which drops from the unique boss **Xyclucian, the Chimera**. Once you have it, you can retry the trial until you succeed.

### How it works

The Trial of Chaos is a series of combat chambers followed by a random boss. Before each chamber you pick **one of three Tribulations**: negative modifiers (hazards, weaker you, stronger enemies) that stay for the rest of the run. Each Tribulation also comes with a reward added to your final winnings, so pick the ones your build handles best. Unlike the Sekhemas there is no Honour: your usual life, flasks and defences keep you alive.

Xyclucian, the boss guarding the first key, attacks with every element plus chaos and physical slams: bring balanced resistances.

## Endgame Points (5 to 8)

Pick the trial that suits your build:

- **Sekhemas** suits builds that avoid hits well (ranged, evasion, mobility) and players who like a careful, roguelike run. Build up Honour resistance on relics before the 3rd and 4th floors.
- **Chaos** suits tanky builds that can take a few hits and kill quickly. For the last two points you need all three Fate fragments: Cowardly Fate, Deadly Fate and Victorious Fate.

Both trials also give their own rewards (Sekhemas treasure keys, Chaos soul cores and more), so they stay worth running after you have all eight points.
