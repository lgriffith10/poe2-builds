The campaign is the tutorial; the Atlas of Worlds is the game. This guide covers the first hours after the campaign, when a lot of new systems open at once.

> **The short version.** Cap your resistances, run low-tier maps to get comfortable, push the Precursor Fortress storyline first (it gives your Atlas passives), and only then specialise in a league mechanic with Tablets.

## Before Your First Map

Your character will be around level 65 when you finish the three Interludes. Run through this checklist from the [beginner's guide](/guides/leveling-and-gearing#arriving-in-maps-the-checklist) first: 75% elemental resistances, 25-30% movement speed, a weapon at your level and good flasks. Map monsters are balanced around capped resistances.

## Unlocking Maps

Enter the Vaal Ziggurat and talk to Doryani to unlock the **map device**. From there you travel across the Atlas of Worlds, a huge map of nodes that keeps expanding as you explore.

**Deaths cost more here.** Each map gives you a limited number of portals. If you die and run out of them, the map's extra objectives are lost and you have to complete the node again. Play a little more carefully than in the campaign.

## Waystones

Each map node needs a **Waystone** to open. They drop from monsters and endgame content, so you will have plenty.

- **Tiers.** You start with tier I. Higher-level monsters can drop the next tier, all the way to XV. Tiers I-V are white, VI-X yellow, XI-XV red. Corrupting a tier XV with a Vaal Orb can give a tier XVI.
- **Modifiers.** Every modifier you add makes monsters harder, but also increases pack size, rarity and quantity of loot. A rare Waystone can have up to 6 modifiers.
- **Bosses.** Nodes marked with a boss icon have a stronger boss that always drops a higher-tier Waystone.

> **Tip.** Don't rush tiers. Moving up when your character clears the current tier comfortably is faster than dying and losing maps.

## Tablets

**Tablets** are added to the map device to add content to a map: a Breach, a Ritual, an Abyss and so on. They work as a loop: the more you use one type, the more of that type you find. Tablets can be crafted too, with up to 4 modifiers.

## The Precursor Fortress (do this first)

While exploring, you will find **Precursor Towers**: completing one reveals a large area around it. The first tower also reveals a nearby **Fortress**.

1. Explore the Fortress and find its three keys.
2. Defeat the **Arbiter of Ash** to open the centre.
3. Inside waits the **Arbiter of Divinity**, the final boss of Path of Exile 2. Your first encounter is a weaker version, to learn the fight.

Maps inside the Fortress end with **Precursor Relays** that give **Atlas passive points**. Beating the Arbiter of Divinity completes a large part of the Fortress at once, with one point per map completed that way. Finishing this storyline also unlocks Citadels and special maps across the Atlas, so it should be your top priority.

## The Atlas Passive Tree

Atlas passives improve your maps. There are 301 main points and you can eventually allocate **all** of them, so there is no wrong order: early on, take what makes your maps more rewarding or safer. Some nodes ask you to pick one option (for example, what powerful map bosses come with).

## Masters of the Atlas

Three masters give you questlines; each completed step unlocks abilities that change your maps. Only one master can be active, with up to three abilities, but switching is free.

| Master | What they offer |
| --- | --- |
| **Jado** | More uniques: extra uniques from map bosses, unique strongboxes, extra modifiers on corrupted Waystones |
| **Hilda** | Monster hunting: more powerful map bosses, Azmeri spirits that go for unique bosses |
| **Doryani** | Waystone tuning: safer maps (an extra revival) or harder, more rewarding ones |

## Picking a League Mechanic

Once the Fortress is done, specialise. Each mechanic has its own Tablets, rewards and pinnacle boss: Breach, Ritual, Delirium, Expedition, Abyss and the Vaal Temple, plus the two trials. A good habit:

1. Try each mechanic a few times with Tablets.
2. Keep the one your build handles well and that you enjoy.
3. Stack its Tablets and Atlas passives to grow its rewards.

For what each mechanic rewards and how to turn loot into currency, see [What to Farm](/guides/farming-and-currency). Sell what you don't use (see [trading](/guides/leveling-and-gearing#trading)), and spend the currency on the upgrades listed in your build guide's Endgame and Min-max gear tabs.
