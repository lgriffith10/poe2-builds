## How It Works

A **Djinn Barya** (dropped by monsters from Act 2, higher level ones give more floors) opens the trial. Place Relics on the Relic Altar, then go through four floors of seven rooms with a boss at the end of each. **Honour** is the trial's own health bar: it equals your life plus energy shield, every hit removes some, and the run ends at zero. Armour reduces the Honour lost per hit, and evasion gives a chance to lose none. Afflictions make the run harder, Boons easier; Sacred Water buys Boons and Relics from merchants.

## What You Get

- **Keys** (bronze, silver, gold) open the reward chests at the end of each floor. Chest types, best first: Spectrum (unique [[Grand Spectrum]] jewels), Time-Lost (Time-Lost Jewels, found only here, which strengthen the passives around their socket), Royal (jewels), Arcanist's (currency), Relic, then random items and equipment.
- **Relics** are permanent upgrades for future runs: more Honour, Honour resistance (aim for 75% in total), more keys or relics, extra rooms revealed.
- Zarokh, the Temporal, at the end of floor four, drops unique Relics that turn the next run into a challenge (no Honour recovery, one Honour…) with an extra reward, including exclusive uniques.

## Making Currency

- Take Key rooms first; spend leftover Sacred Water on extra keys at the Keth Forge before the end.
- Jewels are the bulk of the profit: Time-Lost and Grand Spectrum jewels sell well, so open those chests first.
- Relics with Honour resistance and increased relic quantity sell for a lot; keep the rest on your altar.
- Djinn Barya sell to players who need their ascendancy points, and so do trial carries.

## Farming Strategy

The trial has no Atlas sub-tree: your progression is the Relic altar. Build it towards 75% Honour resistance and more keys, then run Djinn Barya at the highest level you clear reliably. Open Key rooms first and the Spectrum and Time-Lost chests before anything else, and sell the Barya you will not use.

## Which Builds Fit

It suits builds that avoid getting hit: ranged characters, minions and totems that keep enemies off you, with a large life or energy shield pool. Melee builds lose Honour much faster.
