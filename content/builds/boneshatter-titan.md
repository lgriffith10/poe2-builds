## How It Works

Enemies that are primed for stun explode when [[Boneshatter]] hits them, so the core loop is simple: prime packs with [[Leap Slam]] (with Crater it also deals real damage), then Boneshatter the whole group.

For tougher targets, [[Earthshatter]] raises spikes that burst when you use [[Seismic Cry]] or [[Infernal Cry]], and [[Hammer of the Gods]] is the heavy finisher. [[Herald of Ash]] adds fire damage on top.

The Titan's Colossal Capacity, Hulking Form, Stone Skin and Mysterious Lineage add size, armour and defences. The source guide also covers the campaign (Rolling Slam, Mace Strike, Perfect Strike and Shockwave Totem before the main skills arrive).

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** [[Leap Slam]] into a pack, then [[Boneshatter]].

**Bossing.** Use [[Earthshatter]] until the boss is heavy stunned, then [[Seismic Cry]] and [[Infernal Cry]] to detonate the spikes, and drop [[Hammer of the Gods]]. Pre-place spikes while a boss spawns or before opening an essence. Put the warcries on weapon set 2.

## Gear Priorities

A big two-handed mace with physical damage first, then life, armour and resistances. The build needs no uniques; Thrillsteel is a common upgrade.
