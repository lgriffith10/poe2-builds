## How It Works

This page is drawn from the poe.ninja ladder rather than a written guide. [[Earthshatter]] is used by 754 of the 3,374 Titan characters, and its links barely change between players: **Aftershock II (94%), Rapid Attacks II (93%) and Branching Fissures II (92%)**. The character shown adds Life Leech III for sustain.

Half of these players also slam with [[Boneshatter]], and many run Herald of Ice (41%) or Herald of Thunder (19%) to finish packs. This character uses [[Herald of Ice]] and [[Herald of Ash]].

Our [Boneshatter Titan](/builds/boneshatter-titan) guide is a good way to level into this setup: it also uses Earthshatter for bosses.

## Ascendancy and Key Passives

Earthbreaker (95%), Colossal Capacity (94%), Hulking Form (93%) and Ancestral Empowerment (86%) are taken by almost every Titan in this group. Iron Reflexes appears on 48% of them.

## Gear

The most common uniques are Heart of the Well (73%), Megalomaniac (54%), From Nothing (52%) and Morior Invictus (50%). Start with a two-handed mace with high physical damage, life and capped resistances; the uniques come later.
