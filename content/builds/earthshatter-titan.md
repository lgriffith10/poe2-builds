## How It Works

This page is drawn from the poe.ninja ladder rather than a written guide: 782 Titan characters use [[Earthshatter]], and the reference character is level 99. Its links barely change between players: **Aftershock II (93%), Rapid Attacks II (92%) and Branching Fissures II (92%)**. Earthshatter slams a fissure into the ground, Branching Fissures II splits it into secondary fissures and Aftershock II gives it a chance to hit again. The reference character adds Life Leech III for sustain.

Half of these players (49%) also slam with [[Boneshatter]], and many use [[Harbinger of Madness]] (41%), [[Herald of Ice]] (40%) or Herald of Thunder (19%) to finish packs. The reference character runs Herald of Ice and [[Herald of Ash]], with [[War Banner]] for bosses.

The Titan ascendancy:
- **Earthbreaker (94%)** gives slams you use yourself a 25% chance to cause an additional aftershock, which stacks with Aftershock II.
- **Hulking Form (92%)** gives 50% increased effect of small passive skills, so every small node on the tree is worth more.
- **Ancestral Empowerment (84%)** makes every second slam you use Ancestrally Boosted.
- **Colossal Capacity (94%)** adds a chest with 20 inventory slots: a comfort pick for mapping.

## Leveling

No ladder character shows a campaign, so the leveling steps on this page come from Maxroll's Boneshatter Titan leveling guide, which levels with Boneshatter and maces. Our [Boneshatter Titan](/builds/boneshatter-titan) guide also uses Earthshatter for bosses, so moving over is easy. The tree and ascendancy above are endgame choices: follow the leveling guide's tree during the campaign.

Switch to Earthshatter once it has Aftershock II and Branching Fissures II. Take Ancestral Empowerment and Earthbreaker first for damage, Hulking Form next, and Colossal Capacity last.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Slam Earthshatter into packs: the fissures and their branches cover a wide area, and Herald of Ice and Herald of Ash spread the kills. [[Leap Slam]] moves you between packs.

**Bossing.** Plant [[War Banner]], use [[Berserk]] and [[Magma Barrier]], then slam the boss with Earthshatter. Every second slam is Ancestrally Boosted, so keep slamming rather than waiting. [[Raise Shield]] blocks heavy hits.

## Endgame Progression

**First maps.**
- A two-handed mace with high physical damage, life and capped resistances is enough to start. [[Sadist's Mercy]] (46%) is cheap, with physical damage, critical chance and attack speed.
- Lavianga's Spirits (52%) is a cheap unique flask.
- Armour gear with life on every slot: Hulking Form makes the armour and life nodes stronger.

**Endgame.**
- Heart of the Well (73%), Megalomaniac (54%) and From Nothing (53%) are the common jewels.
- [[Morior Invictus]] (51%) is the common body armour, and Rite of Passage (45%) the common charm.
- Iron Reflexes (46%) and Dance with Death (37%) are frequent keystones. Dance with Death needs an empty off-hand and a one-handed weapon, which is why the reference character uses a second weapon set.
- [[Mageblood]] (46%) is a luxury, worn by the reference character but not required.

## Gear Priorities

Physical damage and attack speed on the mace, then life, armour and capped resistances. + to melee skill levels and Spirit on the amulet, added damage to attacks on gloves and rings, and movement speed on boots.

## FAQ

**Why do some slams hit harder?** Ancestral Empowerment boosts every second slam you use yourself, and Earthbreaker adds extra aftershocks.

**Is it fast enough for mapping?** The fissures cover a lot of ground, and the heralds kill the rest. It is still slower than ranged builds, so use Leap Slam to move.

**What is Colossal Capacity for?** It only adds inventory space. Most Titans take it because the other ascendancy notables are already covered.
