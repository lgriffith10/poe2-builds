## How It Works

Until the end of Act 3 you play the same ranged Monk as the [Minion Army Acolyte of Chayula](/builds/minion-army-acolyte-of-chayula) guide: [[Crossbow Shot]] and [[Explosive Grenade]], with [[Frost Bomb]] and [[Flash Grenade]] in support. With spectres unlocked, Vaal Guards take over, then Zealot spectres that cast Spark from early mapping on.

The permanent army is [[Skeletal Warrior]], [[Skeletal Storm Mage]], [[Skeletal Cleric]] and [[Wolf Pack]]. [[Pain Offering]] and [[Blasphemy]] strengthen the army from the back line, and [[Into the Breach]] keeps Remnants coming.

## Playstyle

**Clear.** Advance and let the spectres throw Spark through the pack.

**Single target.** Keep the curse from [[Blasphemy]] on the boss, refresh [[Pain Offering]], and use [[Refutation]] for big hits.

## Defences

Energy shield and evasion, with Wolf Pack's damage reduction and [[Refutation]] to block in dangerous moments. [[Convalescence]] adds recovery.

## Gear Priorities

+Level of all Minion Skills and spirit first. [[Alpha's Howl]] is the key helmet for mapping, with [[Bones of Ullr]] boots and [[Prism Guardian]] on the second weapon set.
