## How It Works

[[Entangle]] covers the ground in vines. [[Thunderstorm]] then "waters" them: with [[Accelerated Growth II]] linked to [[Entangle]], the vines grow out of control and burst. [[Thrashing Vines]] summons larger vines that slam enemies and handle tough targets.

The Disciple of Varashta brings two Djinns. [[Ruzhan, the Blazing Sword]] adds fire damage and [[Kelari, the Tainted Sands]] breaks armour, so both keep fighting at your side while you cast. [[Mana Tempest]], [[Mana Remnants]] and [[Sigil of Power]] boost your spells during big fights.

## Playstyle

**Clear.** Cast [[Entangle]] into the pack, then [[Thunderstorm]] on top. Most packs die in the explosion.

**Single target.**

1. Place [[Sigil of Power]].
2. Summon [[Thrashing Vines]] next to the boss.
3. Cast [[Thunderstorm]], then keep [[Entangle]] under the boss.

## Defences

Early on you play a life and energy shield hybrid with armour. Later the source guide switches to Chaos Inoculation to stack energy shield, which works with the ascendancy's own defences. [[Blasphemy]] with [[Temporal Chains]] slows everything around you, and [[Convalescence]] adds recovery.

## Gear Priorities

+Level of all Spell Skills and cast speed on the staff, then energy shield and resistances everywhere else. [[Bushwhack]] boots are a great early pick: your physical damage pins enemies in place. [[Idol of Uldurn]] is a good campaign amulet. The late Chaos Inoculation setup uses [[Headhunter]], which is optional and very expensive.
