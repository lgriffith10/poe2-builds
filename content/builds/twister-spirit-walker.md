## How It Works

[[Twister]] throws spinning whirlwinds that travel across the screen and hit everything in their path. [[Whirling Slash]] creates a whirlwind around you that grows over successive slashes, and Twisters thrown while it is active are empowered by it. The build converts that into cold damage: [[Frost Nexus]] leaves chilled ground behind, [[Ice-Tipped Arrows]] spray icy fragments, and [[Herald of Ice]] makes frozen enemies explode.

Spirit Walker adds a permanent companion through [[Wild Protector]] and the [[Vivid Stampede]] skill, while Primal Bounty is the first ascendancy pick for its projectile scaling.

## Playstyle

**Clearing.** Throw [[Twister]] and let [[Ice-Tipped Arrows]] freeze the pack and lay chilled ground. Most trash dies to the icy fragments alone. Only slash with [[Whirling Slash]] when a rare is tanky; on very dense packs, three slashes followed by [[Barrage]] clears off-screen.

**Bossing.** Mark the boss with [[Freezing Mark]], drop [[War Banner]], build a full [[Whirling Slash]] whirlwind, then use [[Barrage]] and unload empowered Twisters. Keep moving between casts; your companion holds aggro.

## Defences

Like most Huntress builds, defences come from Evasion and Deflection on every armour slot, plus [[Wind Dancer]]. Keep resistances capped and avoid standing in ground effects.

## Endgame Progression

**Weapon sets.** Weapon set 1 always wants the most attack speed for Whirling Slash. Weapon set 2 wants the most flat damage prefixes, not the highest displayed DPS (which attack speed inflates): ideally three flat damage prefixes, preferably physical and cold, with high critical chance as a suffix. + levels to Projectile Skills are also very strong. An Omen Sceptre in set 2 as soon as possible adds Malice for critical chance (it drops in level 16+ zones).

**Early maps.**
- Evasion and deflection on body armour, gloves, boots and helmet are critical to your defences.
- A Valako's Roar charm is cheap and common, and adds frenzy charge generation.

**Endgame uniques.**
- **The Taming** is very expensive but gives your Twisters all three ground effects instead of one, so you no longer create ground effects yourself and Frost Nexus's support slot is free.
- **The Sacred Flame** adds a large chunk of damage as extra fire, and makes an enemy's lowest resistance count for all its elemental resistances. Monsters often have only one or two resistances, so this largely removes the need for penetration.
- **Lavianga's Spirits** keeps mana flowing and keeps Stimulants and Imbibed Power always active; the source guide's aspirational tree has swaps for when you get it.

## Gear Priorities

The spear matters most: +Level of all Projectile Skills, added cold and physical damage, attack speed and critical strike chance. An amulet with Spirit and projectile levels comes next, then life, evasion and Deflection on armour. Later upgrades from the source guide's aspirational setup include [[The Black Insignia]], [[The Taming]] and [[Sacred Flame]].

## FAQ

**Ice-Tipped Arrows doesn't seem to work, what's wrong?** [[Ice-Tipped Arrows]] must be on weapon set 2 with [[Twister]]. Only [[Barrage]] and [[Whirling Slash]] belong on weapon set 1.

**Why use two weapon sets?** Weapon set 1 maximises attack speed for [[Whirling Slash]] (and skill speed for Barrage through Dance with Death), while weapon set 2 maximises [[Twister]] damage, for example with an Omen Sceptre for extra critical chance. Put [[War Banner]] on set 1 to save Spirit.

**When should I switch to the crit tree?** Around the mid 80s, once you own a spear with good local critical chance. Base critical chance matters a lot, so do not switch before you have both the levels and the spear.

**I feel like I'm dying too much, what do I do?** Cap elemental resistances at 75%, aim for roughly 60% chaos resistance, and use bases that give evasion and energy shield in the right slots. Do not start [[Whirling Slash]] while fully surrounded: move somewhere safer first.
