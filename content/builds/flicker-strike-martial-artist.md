## How It Works

This page is drawn from the poe.ninja ladder rather than a written guide. [[Flicker Strike]] teleports you to an enemy and strikes it, then repeats; players link it with **Elemental Armament II (94%), Concentrated Area (79%), Close Combat II (78%) and Hit and Run (69%)**.

[[Charged Staff]] (87%) adds lightning to your strikes, and Heralds are common: [[Herald of Ice]] (63%) and [[Herald of Thunder]] (56%). [[Falling Thunder]] (60%) and [[Whirling Assault]] (50%) appear on many characters.

## Ascendancy and Key Passives

Way of the Stonefist (99%), Runic Meridians (92%), Martial Adept (78%) and Martial Master (76%). Chaos Inoculation is taken by 54% of the group.

## Gear

Nascent Hope (65%) and Beira's Anguish (36%) are common. This character wears [[Polcirkeln]]; [[Morior Invictus]] and [[Mageblood]] are endgame luxuries.
