## How It Works

This page is drawn from the poe.ninja ladder rather than a written guide: 2,356 Martial Artist characters use [[Flicker Strike]], and the reference character is level 99. Flicker Strike teleports you to an enemy and hits it, so moving and attacking are the same button. Players link it with **Elemental Armament II (94%)**, Concentrated Area (79%), Close Combat II (77%) and Hit and Run (69%); Rakiata's Flow (54%) makes its hits treat enemy elemental resistances as inverted.

The damage is elemental: [[Herald of Ice]] (62%) and [[Herald of Thunder]] (57%) explode packs, and [[Charged Staff]] (87%) adds lightning to your staff attacks. [[Whirling Assault]] (51%) and Falling Thunder (59%) are the usual second attacks for bosses. [[Polcirkeln]] on the reference character lets chilled enemies shatter as if frozen.

The Martial Artist ascendancy:
- **Way of the Stonefist (99%)** turns your gloves into Fists of Stone and makes their modifiers more powerful.
- **Runic Meridians (92%)** lets you tattoo runes onto your body: extra rune-only sockets on the helmet, body armour, gloves and boots.
- **Martial Adept (78%)** gives an extra Combo each time you gain one, and shortens energy shield recharge delay as you spend Combo.
- **Martial Master (76%)** builds Combo from every attack hit, on either weapon set.

## Leveling

No ladder character shows a campaign, so the leveling steps on this page come from Maxroll's Storm Wave Monk leveling guide, a quarterstaff route with Glacial Cascade, Storm Wave and the heralds. The tree and ascendancy above are endgame choices: follow the leveling guide's tree during the campaign.

Flicker Strike comes later than the leveling skills: switch once you have it with Elemental Armament II and the heralds. Take Way of the Stonefist and Runic Meridians first, then Martial Adept and Martial Master. Chaos Inoculation (53%) is a later respec, once energy shield carries your defences.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Hold Flicker Strike: you jump from enemy to enemy and the heralds clear around you. Dodge roll or stop pressing to choose where to go next.

**Bossing.** Keep Charged Staff up, then use Whirling Assault or Falling Thunder on the boss and Flicker Strike to reposition out of danger. [[Wind Dancer]] adds evasion while you fight.

## Endgame Progression

**First maps.**
- A quarterstaff with critical chance and elemental damage, + to melee skill levels on the amulet, and added cold and lightning damage to attacks on rings.
- Evasion and energy shield gear with capped resistances. Socket runes in the extra sockets from Runic Meridians.

**Endgame.**
- Heart of the Well (77%) is the common jewel, Nascent Hope (64%), Rite of Passage (57%) and Beira's Anguish (35%) the common charms, and Lavianga's Spirits (56%) the common flask.
- [[Morior Invictus]] (42%) on the body and [[Kalandra's Touch]] (30%) on a ring.
- Chaos Inoculation (53%) and Hollow Focus Technique (22%) are frequent keystone choices.
- [[Mageblood]] (54%) is a luxury, worn by the reference character but not required.

## Gear Priorities

Elemental damage and critical chance on the quarterstaff, + to melee skill levels, then evasion, energy shield and capped resistances. Added elemental damage to attacks on rings and gloves, and movement speed on boots.

## FAQ

**How do I control where Flicker Strike goes?** It picks a nearby enemy. Stop pressing to stay put, and dodge roll out of anything dangerous.

**Do I need Chaos Inoculation?** 53% of these players take it. Respec only once your energy shield is high enough.

**What are tattooed runes?** Runic Meridians adds rune-only sockets on your armour pieces, so you socket more runes than the item normally allows.
