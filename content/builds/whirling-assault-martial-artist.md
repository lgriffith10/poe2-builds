## How It Works

[[Whirling Assault]] is a channelled quarterstaff attack: you spin forward and hit everything around you as you move. Martial Artist's Hollow Focus Technique lets [[Hollow Focus]] empower the skill, and the rest of the ascendancy plus the tree push critical strike chance and damage. [[Rend]], [[Devour]] and [[Charged Staff]] keep charges flowing, and marks from [[Hand of Chayula]] weaken bosses.

## Leveling

[[Whirling Assault]] is not available until much later in the campaign, so the source guide levels with cold spells:

- **Early game:** place [[Frozen Locus]] and detonate it from range with [[Glacial Cascade]]. Almost all of Glacial Cascade's damage is in the large final spike, so aim that spike at the Locus. Add [[Herald of Ice]] around level 10.
- **From level 22:** detonate [[Frozen Locus]] with [[Storm Wave]] instead. On bosses, apply exposure with [[Frost Bomb]], build up the freeze, then lock it in with [[Wave of Frost]] before consuming it.
- **Once Whirling Assault is unlocked:** switch to the quarterstaff setup above.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Keep [[Hollow Focus]] and your buffs up and channel [[Whirling Assault]] through packs; [[Herald of Ash]] spreads fire damage from kills.

**Bossing.** Mark the boss with [[Hand of Chayula]], refresh [[Charged Staff]] and [[Rend]], add [[Tempest Bell]], then channel [[Whirling Assault]] in melee range while dodging telegraphed attacks. [[Ghost Dance]] and [[Wind Dancer]] help you survive the exchanges.

## Gear Priorities

The quarterstaff comes first: physical damage, critical strike chance, +Level of all Melee Skills and attack speed. Then an amulet with +Level of all Melee Skills, gloves with added physical damage and critical damage, and hybrid evasion/energy shield armour with resistances.
