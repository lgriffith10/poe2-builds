## How It Works

[[Falling Thunder]] consumes power charges for a huge lightning strike. The build revolves around generating those charges and spending them at the right moment. [[Devour]] eats corpses for charges, [[Hollow Focus]] spawns bells that can be devoured for an instant three charges, and [[Rend]] spends a charge for a temporary damage bonus. In maps, [[Herald of Thunder]] and [[Whirling Assault]] handle most of the clear.

Martial Artist's Runic Meridians lets you socket five extra runes; the source guide uses them for resistances, attributes and utility runes.

## Leveling

Clear with [[Frozen Locus]] and [[Herald of Ice]], detonating the Locus crystal with Quarterstaff Strike, Wind Blast or [[Storm Wave]] as you progress. For bosses, build power charges and spend them on [[Falling Thunder]].

## Playstyle

**Clear.** Let the Heralds chain through packs; in big encounters such as Abyss, [[Devour]] corpses to stock up on power charges.

**Bossing.**

1. [[Devour]] the [[Hollow Focus]] bells before the fight for three power charges.
2. Apply [[Frost Bomb]] for exposure and keep [[Rend]]'s bonus active.
3. Wait out the boss's short damage reduction window at the start of the fight, then spend charges on [[Falling Thunder]].
4. Cull enemies with [[Devour]] or [[Killing Palm]] to trigger Thrill of the Kill.

## Gear Priorities

Quarterstaff damage and critical strike first, then hybrid evasion and energy shield armour. [[Shavronne's Satchel]] is the source guide's belt.

## FAQ

**How do I generate power charges?** It changes as the build develops: [[Wing Blast]] and [[Killing Palm]] in Act 1, [[Siphoning Strike]] and [[Devour]] from Act 2, [[Reaper's Invocation]] with [[Profane Ritual]] in early maps, then [[Ailith's Chimes]] and Martial Master in the endgame.

**How do I get more critical hit chance?** The build's damage depends on it. Sources: the Deadly Force, Critical Exploit, Moment of Truth and Struck Through notables, a suffix on the helmet and on the amulet, a Sinister Quarterstaff (the highest base critical chance), Fists of Stone gloves (Way of the Stonefist turns their critical damage bonus modifier into base critical chance), [[Charge Regulation]] (20% more critical chance with a power charge), and the [[Blindside]] and [[Pinpoint Critical]] supports.

**How do I raise my defences?** Cap your elemental resistances and build high energy shield, evasion and deflection, plus high life rolls on the early variants. Energy shield recovery comes from [[Shavronne's Satchel]] and an instant-recovery Ultimate Life Flask.
