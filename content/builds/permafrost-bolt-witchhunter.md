## How It Works

[[Permafrost Bolts]] can be used from level 1, so you play the endgame build from the start. Both it and [[Fragmentation Rounds]] fire a spread of bolts, so they hit hardest at close range. Fragmentation Rounds only shine on frozen enemies, but thanks to its built-in pierce you only need one frozen enemy in a pack: the explosions spread to other frozen enemies and chain through the group. [[Shard Scavenger]] then reloads your crossbow instantly after Fragmentation Rounds.

Witchhunter's Pitiless Killer and Judge, Jury, and Executioner speed up kills, while Obsessive Rituals and Ceremonial Ablution add defences.

## Weapon Swap Setup

1. Lock your crossbow to both weapon sets by right-clicking the Weapon Set I indicator.
2. Put [[Permafrost Bolts]] on set 1, and [[Fragmentation Rounds]] and [[Explosive Grenade]] on set 2.
3. Bind Crossbow Shot I and II to separate keys and load each ammunition once.

You can now fire both in sequence without reloading.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Early on, freeze with Permafrost Bolts and finish with Fragmentation Rounds. Later, Permafrost Bolts clear on their own and you save Fragmentation Rounds for rares.

**Bossing.** Apply [[Freezing Mark]], freeze the boss with Permafrost Bolts, then unload Fragmentation Rounds. [[Wind Dancer]] knocks back enemies that hit you.
