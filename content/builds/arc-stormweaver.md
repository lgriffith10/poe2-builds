## How It Works

[[Arc]] is both the clear and the single-target skill: it chains between enemies. [[Orb of Storms]] adds damage on its own and generates Lightning Infusions that greatly amplify Arc. On weapon set 1, [[Mana Tempest]] grants extra lightning damage and makes spells split to more targets, at the cost of a higher mana drain inside its radius; pick up remnants from [[Mana Remnants]] to sustain it. On weapon set 2, [[Frost Bomb]] applies exposure (link Long Fuse and Prolonged Duration to apply more of it) and [[Elemental Weakness]] lowers resistances further.

Stormweaver's Strike Twice, Constant Gale and Storm's Recollection complete the setup.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Place [[Orb of Storms]] and cast [[Arc]] into packs.

**Bossing.** Curse with [[Elemental Weakness]], drop [[Frost Bomb]], activate [[Mana Tempest]] and [[Sigil of Power]], then alternate [[Orb of Storms]] and [[Arc]].

## Endgame Progression

**After the campaign.** Check vendors and the gambling vendor to replace weak items, first to cap resistances, then for more energy shield. Short on gold? Run low-tier maps, loot rares and use Transmutation and Augmentation Orbs on normal bases; in trade leagues, many upgrades cost a few Exalted Orbs each.

**Early maps.**
- A wand with + to Level of Lightning or Spell Skills, increased Lightning or Spell Damage and Damage as Extra Elemental. A Voltaic Staff still works, but a good wand and focus pair ends up stronger.
- An amulet with + to Level of all Spell Skills, ideally a Solar Amulet, instilled with Heavy Ammunition. Rings with cast speed, mana and resistances.
- Archmage needs +60 Spirit from amulet and body armour (+51 at 20% quality). Until then, run Arctic Armour or Convalescence for safety, or Attrition for single-target damage.
- At 6,000+ energy shield, take Chaos Inoculation and drop life affixes. Aim for 100% increased item rarity early, and 25-35% movement speed boots.
- Charms: Thawing (freeze), Dousing (ignite), then Golden for rarity or Stone for stun.
- Gems: equip Archmage as soon as possible and stack mana for its damage. 5-link Arc with a Greater Jeweller's Orb and 4-link everything else.

**Endgame.**
- Arbiter's Ignition linked to Living Bomb generates Elemental Archon, so Archon of the Storm can go. 6-link Arc with a Perfect Jeweller's Orb, 5-link the rest.
- Wand with more + levels, socketed with Hedgewitch Assandra's Rune of Wisdom. Amulet instilled with Lightning Rod, 20% quality from Neural Catalysts.
- Rings with mana from Perfect Essence of the Mind. Helmet with 300+ energy shield, critical chance, the desecrated "increased effect of Arcane Surge on You" modifier and a Soul Core of Zalatl.
- From Nothing (Eldritch Battery) saves many passive points: high priority.
- Lavianga's Spirits removes mana flask spam, but needs 30%+ critical chance to keep generating Mana Remnants.

**Aspirational and min-max.** Breach rings for maximum mana, a focus with + Spell levels and mana, Headhunter for clear and attributes, a Megalomaniac with useful notables (Frazzled, The Spring Hare, Calibration, Momentum), Dialla's Desire on Archmage and Uhtred's Augury on Mana Remnants. Min-max moves to Atziri's Splendour and sanctified, corrupted items. The source guide calls the aspirational set a league-long goal.

## Gear Priorities

The source guide eventually six-links Arc with a Perfect Jeweller's Orb and five-links the other skills. Gear focuses on spell levels, cast speed and energy shield.

## FAQ

**I don't have enough Spirit for all my persistent buffs, what should I do?** After the campaign, [[Archmage]] needs a total of +60 to maximum Spirit from your amulet and body armour, usually a body armour with +47 Spirit or more plus any Solar Amulet. At 20% quality, Archmage only needs +51, which a body armour alone can provide. Until then, run [[Arctic Armour]] or [[Convalescence]] for survivability, or [[Attrition]] if you lack single target damage.

**How do I improve my mana sustain?** Cost efficiency is the strongest fix: take Efficient Casting and Conservative Casting on the tree, or temporarily link [[Efficiency II]] if your damage can spare a link. If remnants run short in boss fights, take Storm's Recollection as your third ascendancy point, aim for 30% or more critical hit chance, and consider Arcane Remnants and Empowering Remnants. If [[Mana Tempest]] is the drain, only activate it after you have picked up several [[Mana Remnants]]. Force of Will as the fourth ascendancy point, pressing a mana flask, mana regeneration and mana on kill (for clearing) are the next options.

**How do I get more Lightning Infusions?** Make sure [[Orb of Storms]] is linked to [[Harmonic Remnants II]]. Refracted Infusion (fourth ascendancy point) makes [[Frost Bomb]], [[Living Bomb]] and [[Siphon Elements]] generate many more, and the Infusing Power, Everlasting Infusions, Shocking Limit and Infused Limits notables add more or keep them longer. If a boss is slow to appear, pre-cast Orb of Storms and let it expire so you start the fight with maximum infusions.

**What combo mistakes cost the most damage?** Skills on the wrong weapon set, not walking over Lightning Infusions and Mana Remnants, activating Mana Tempest too early, skipping [[Sigil of Power]], letting [[Orb of Storms]] or [[Frost Bomb]] lapse, and mistiming [[Elemental Weakness]]. Elemental Archon should be triggered with Elemental Weakness early on and with [[Living Bomb]] later.

**How do I die less?** Your defences are a large energy shield pool, staying at range, and Heavy Snows (which needs gain as extra cold damage) to slow monsters. Weave in and out of combat so energy shield can recharge, and take more faster start of energy shield recharge if needed. Upgrade armour bases for energy shield, cap elemental resistances, and carry the charm that matches the threat (resistance, Amethyst for chaos, Stone for stuns, Thawing for freeze, Dousing for ignites, Staunching for bleeds).
