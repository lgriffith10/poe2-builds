## How It Works

[[Arc]] is both the clear and the single-target skill: it chains between enemies. [[Orb of Storms]] adds damage on its own and generates Lightning Infusions that greatly amplify Arc. On weapon set 1, [[Mana Tempest]] grants extra lightning damage and makes spells split to more targets, at the cost of a higher mana drain inside its radius; pick up remnants from [[Mana Remnants]] to sustain it. On weapon set 2, [[Frost Bomb]] applies exposure (link Long Fuse and Prolonged Duration to apply more of it) and [[Elemental Weakness]] lowers resistances further.

Stormweaver's Strike Twice, Constant Gale and Storm's Recollection complete the setup.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Place [[Orb of Storms]] and cast [[Arc]] into packs.

**Bossing.** Curse with [[Elemental Weakness]], drop [[Frost Bomb]], activate [[Mana Tempest]] and [[Sigil of Power]], then alternate [[Orb of Storms]] and [[Arc]].

## Gear Priorities

The source guide eventually six-links Arc with a Perfect Jeweller's Orb and five-links the other skills. Gear focuses on spell levels, cast speed and energy shield.
