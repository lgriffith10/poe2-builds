## How It Works

This page is drawn from the poe.ninja ladder rather than a written guide. [[Twister]] sends tornadoes through packs; players link it with **Projectile Acceleration III (98%) and Elemental Armament II (92%)**, then Rakiata's Flow (53%) and Salvo (50%).

[[Barrage]] and [[Whirling Slash]] (90% each) support it, [[Called Shots]] (83%) is used by most players, and Heralds are common: [[Herald of Thunder]] (58%) and [[Herald of Ice]] (54%). [[The Taming]] (93%) lets wind skills be boosted by several elemental ground surfaces.

## Ascendancy and Key Passives

Gathering Winds (93%), Called Shots (86%), Avidity (80%) and Thrilling Chase (74%). Dance with Death (81%) is the main keystone.

## Gear

[[The Taming]], Lavianga's Spirits (83%) and [[Sacred Flame]] (65%) are the most common uniques. This character also wears [[Yoke of Suffering]]; [[Mageblood]] is an endgame luxury.
