## How It Works

[[Whirling Slash]] creates a wind vortex that stacks up to three times; [[Twister]] cast inside it consumes the vortex for more damage and more projectiles. Twister is your damage, Whirling Slash only builds vortices. [[Ice-Tipped Arrows]] imbues your next four projectiles, converting their damage to cold and creating icy explosions. [[Barrage]] consumes Frenzy Charges to add more Twister projectiles.

The Deadeye's Avidity generates Frenzy Charges, and Called Shots passively applies [[Sniper's Mark]] to generate more. Gathering Winds and Thrilling Chase add speed. Defence mixes high evasion with deflection, and energy shield in the endgame setups. No unique is required.

## Weapon Sets

Weapon sets are extremely important: allocate every set-specific passive point as the guide's tree shows.
- **Set 1** is for Whirling Slash: as much attack speed as possible, with the off-hand empty for Dance with Death and Rigwald's Ferocity. A Winged or Soaring Spear with an attack speed suffix, or Skysliver.
- **Set 2** is for Twister: as much damage as possible. On the spear, increased and added physical damage, critical chance and + Projectile levels. Switch from a buckler to a sceptre, preferably an Omen Sceptre, after the second Uncut Spirit Gem (level 6).

## Leveling

- **Act 1.** The only way to make elemental ground is Fangs of Frost: Parry with the buckler, then Fangs of Frost on the primed enemy for a counter that leaves chilled ground. Plant [[War Banner]] on bosses.
- **Act 2.** Frost Nexus handles chilled ground, so drop Fangs of Frost. Use Ice-Tipped Arrows even while clearing to freeze regularly. Cull the Weak gives mobility and Frenzy Charges while moving. Cast Freezing Mark manually on rares and bosses (marking a weak monster and freezing it also grants the cold damage buff for 10 seconds).
- **Act 3 onward.** [[Herald of Ice]] helps clear a lot; give it supports. Armour helps in Acts 1-3 against knockdowns while parrying; move to evasion from Act 3.
- Flat damage to attacks on jewellery and gloves, life and resistances on armour.

## Playstyle

**Basic rotation.** Ice-Tipped Arrows when off cooldown, Whirling Slash three times, Barrage when off cooldown, then Twister. You do not always need three slashes; you will get a feel for it.

**Bossing.** Once Called Shots applies the mark, proc it twice as soon as you can for up to 4 Frenzy Charges; in a pack it often jumps to a new target. With Salvo, wait for at least 4 seals before releasing the empowered Twister and Barrage.

## Endgame Progression

**Called Shots.** After the fourth ascendancy, Called Shots casts marks automatically. Switch it to Sniper's Mark once you have a Rune of Consistency and Greater Jeweller's Orbs for Whirling Slash and Barrage, and support Whirling Slash with Pinpoint Critical. Spare Spirit: Herbalism I and/or Clarity I.

**Key items.**
- **The Taming** is best in slot and the top priority: 100% of damage as extra elemental, and it always empowers Twister whether or not you stand on elemental ground. Early on, Berek's Grip, Berek's Pass or Berek's Respite. Wake of Destruction generates elemental ground early and replaces Frost Nexus.
- **Olroth's Conviction** is the most important item to get as soon as possible: Barrage three times per cooldown, so Salvo can leave Twister. Pair it with Uhtred's Constellation. Barrage with Olroth's Conviction needs about 200 Ward: Runeforge gloves and boots.
- Serpent's Egg doubles Frenzy Charge generation with solid stats.
- Garukhan's Resolve is by far the strongest lineage support for damage, and Rigwald's Ferocity in weapon set 1 adds a lot of Whirling Slash attack speed. Trinity is a big damage multiplier. A Spear Stab with Ailith's Chimes generates Power Charges on demand for Charge Regulation: build combo with Whirling Slash, then tap Spear Stab.

**Gear.**
- A high physical damage spear (the base matters little early on); The Ordained with at least 10% critical chance before an endgame rare.
- Off-hand: Guiding Palm of the Eye, of the Heart or of the Mind, whichever balances Trinity best; The Sacred Flame for the most damage. With The Sacred Flame and Rakiata's Flow, make sure the boss is outside your presence when casting Twister, as they counteract each other.
- Evasion bases and deflection; "Gain Deflection Rating equal to % of Evasion Rating" wherever you can. Energy shield and evasion on helmet and body armour.
- Jewels: mana on kill for clear, skill effect duration. Flat damage on jewellery stays good, especially with a weak weapon.
- Luxury: Kolr's Hunt marksman gloves, Runeforged Uhtred's Sidereus Sekhema Sandals for skill effect duration, Mageblood with double Silver, Jade or Bismuth (Diamond or Amethyst otherwise), and Headhunter for content with many rares if you can keep resistances capped.

## Gear Priorities

Attack speed on the weapon set 1 spear, damage on the weapon set 2 spear, The Taming and Olroth's Conviction, flat damage on jewellery and gloves, then evasion, deflection, life and resistances.

## FAQ

**Why keep Whirling Slash at level 1?** It deals negligible damage and becomes your movement in the endgame, so you want its mana cost as low as possible.

**What happens if I leave the vortex?** It collapses for a small amount of area damage, and Twister loses the empowerment: cast Twister while inside it.
