## How It Works

[[Tame Beast]] captures a giant flying wasp (a Plague Harvester or Gargantuan Wasp), and [[Infernal Legion II]] makes it burn enemies in a large radius around it. When those enemies die, [[Blasphemy]] enables Rupture the Soul to make them explode, and since a minion's damage over time counts as your kill, the explosions use your own increased damage and area. [[Frost Bomb]] and Elemental Weakness lower enemy resistances, Sniper's Mark shocks, and [[Spearfield]] aggravates the ignite on bosses.

Defences combine energy shield and evasion, with [[Ghost Dance]] and [[Convalescence]] for recovery, then Chaos Inoculation in the endgame. Crystalline Phylactery adds two jewel sockets worth of stats (Potent Liquid Ferocity is multiplicative with it), and Necromantic Conduit and Blackened Heart give your companions Unholy Might: 30% of damage as extra chaos, which also feeds Rupture the Soul. No unique is required; Blackflame is cheap and excellent, and Xoph's Pyre takes the build further.

## Leveling

- **Acts 1-2.** [[Skeletal Sniper]] (its command arrow leaves a gas cloud that ignited enemies detonate) and [[Flame Wall]] to ignite. Contagion and Unearth early, Frost Bomb for Exposure. After the King in the Mists, cut Raging Spirits and keep about 10 up; [[Pain Offering]] on bosses.
- **Acts 3-4.** At level 31, cut [[Skeletal Reaver]] and summon as many as possible with one Sniper; swap Minion Splash I for Muster on bosses. In weapon set 2, [[Pounce]] applies Predator's Mark (up to 45% increased damage taken), with Opening Move for fast stuns: no minions summoned and the boss at full life. A Skeletal Storm Mage for Shock only if it replaces one Reaver.
- **The Infernal Legion swap** at level 58, with at least 18 weapon set points and about 34,000 gold (identify and sell rares, do not gamble). Never use Infernal Legion I. Capture both companions **before** the respec, with your minions unsummoned: a Plague Harvester with "Extra Fire Damage" (two spawn by a checkpoint in Khari Crossing, north-east of the Khari Bazaar; do not kill them), and a Diretusk Boar with "All Damage Shocks" at the Troubled Camp in the Infested Barrens (Act 3). Do not chase perfect modifiers yet.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Walk through packs and let the wasp burn them; Rupture the Soul does the rest.

**Bossing.** Let the companion reach the boss to ignite it first (the ignite persists when you swap weapons), then Frost Bomb and Sniper's Mark, then Spearfield to aggravate the ignite (it is a chance: the boss takes about twice the damage once it works) and to trigger Sniper's Mark, whose shocked ground you can check on the boss. You can pre-cast Frost Bomb where the boss spawns.

## Companions

**Where to find them.** Respawning at a campaign checkpoint rerolls the modifiers of guaranteed rare beasts: the best method, for the Plague Harvester (Khari Crossing) and Diretusk Boar (Troubled Camp). For other beasts, reset a zone where they spawn (Ctrl+click the entrance or waypoint) until an Essence monster of the right type is near a checkpoint, then respawn: Gargantuan Wasps spawn in the Ashen Forest. In maps, Trenches always has a Gargantuan Wasp: use Hilda's Breeding Season, a Forest biome with Evolutionary Pressures, Rare Beast Modifiers Chance and Apex Predators, and 60%+ monster rarity Waystones; do not complete the map so you can rerun it. Always carry a +1 corrupted Tame Beast with five supports, and open the Market panel to pause and read a beast's modifiers.

**Modifiers.** On the main companion, "All Damage Ignites" is by far the most important. Extra Chaos, Cold or Lightning Damage are then equivalent to Extra Fire: 40% of damage as extra. On the support companion, "All Damage Shocks" (enemies take 20% increased damage, 30% with Overcharge, plus a separate 20% more from Stormfire). Haste Aura (20% attack and cast speed, 10% movement speed) is excellent but cannot roll on fast beasts such as wasps. Elemental Resistance Aura gives 30% resistances in a small radius: good early, not to rely on later.

**The endgame setup** drops the support companion for one Gargantuan Wasp and one Plague Harvester. Avoid Starlight Harvesters (their entrance animation stops damage and breaks Shroud Walker). The Elephant Tortoise has 75% more life, so 75% more DPS, but locks itself in place near enemies unless you keep commanding it or swapping weapons; most players prefer Shroud Walker wasps for clear.

**Fire resistance.** Allocate Vile Mending, Living Death, Minion Fire Resistance and Unspoken Bond to cap your companions' fire resistance against Infernal Legion's self-burn; a Shrine Sceptre with Purity of Fire helps until then.

## Endgame Progression

**Early maps.**
- Energy shield and evasion first, life second. Cap elemental and chaos resistances (a Soul Core of Tacati helps).
- Saitha's Spear in weapon set 2 is cheap and very strong: buy one that kept "(25-40)% chance to Aggravate Bleeding on Hit" and "Aggravating any Bleeding with this Weapon also Aggravates all Ignites". Aggravated ignites deal 100% more damage.
- A +1 minion levels Trenchtimbre replaces the shield (a +1 roll is cheap). Blackflame gives enemies under Withering Presence 90% increased damage taken. Omen of Putrefaction is an early route to a +2 minion helmet. The sceptre's modifiers matter far more than its base.

**Red maps.**
- Energy shield over life, a +2 minion Trenchtimbre, and minion belts and rings from the Genesis Tree (increased minion area of effect matters most on the belt).
- A Bear Idol adds generic area of effect for bigger Rupture the Soul explosions. Instill Warlord Leader on the amulet. Greater Desert Runes fill resistance gaps.

**Endgame.**
- Chaos Inoculation: life and chaos resistance stop mattering. The sceptre must be a Purity of Fire Shrine Sceptre, or allocate Living Death.
- A focus with +2 minion levels replaces the Trenchtimbre for a lot of energy shield, with an Ancient Rune of Witchcraft for a bigger Blasphemy. A second focus for weapon set 2 scales Frost Bomb's Exposure; until then, right-click the lock icon to copy your focus over.
- "Minions have increased Magnitude of Damaging Ailments" on rings and sceptre is huge. Gigantic Following anointed frees the Hulking Minions slot. Cadigan's Epiphany adds a jewel in the gloves. Lavianga's Spirits offsets Necromantic Conduit's mana drain.
- Transcendent Limbs (deflection, presence area, evasion, stun threshold) cost nothing as long as you do not die, but respawning at a checkpoint removes them: get them after farming your companions.

**Min-max.** Mageblood with Jade, Stibnite and Quicksilver (Gold if your resistances stay at 95% each for Exposure, Bismuth if they do not), "Gain Deflection equal to % of Evasion Rating" on gloves, boots and body armour, Jiquani's Thesis, a Raven-Touched Shard for a second instilled notable, and Sacrificial Regalia for +1 to corrupted gems (its physical-as-chaos conversion actually makes you tankier to physical hits; craft it with Astrid's Creativity).

+ minion levels and "Minions have increased Magnitude of Damaging Ailments" on sceptre and rings, energy shield and evasion on armour, then companion damage. "Companions deal increased Damage" scales Rupture the Soul directly through Inspiring Ally, better than generic minion or presence damage.

## FAQ

**Why Chaos Inoculation over Eternal Life?** Eternal Life with Soulless Form and Heavy Buffer (15% damage reduction) is used while progressing. Chaos immunity is huge in content like Ritual and frees gear suffixes and ascendancy points for more damage and defence.

**Why are some explosions bigger?** When your companion kills with its ignite, you are the source of the kill, so Rupture the Soul uses your increased damage and area and your on-kill effects. Kills from its basic attack use its own, much larger minion area of effect.
