## How It Works

You fight in bear form. [[Maul]] builds Rage (5 per hit) and [[Furious Slam]] spends 10 for an empowered slam; Rage itself gives 1% more attack damage per stack. From level 52, [[Walking Calamity]] drops fiery meteors around you on its own once you have 50 Glory, which you gain at full Rage. [[Savage Fury]] and [[Berserk]] add big damage on top, and [[Herald of Ash]] spreads fire through packs.

The Shaman's Furious Wellspring regenerates Rage based on your mana regeneration and stops Rage from decaying, so every fight starts at full power. Reactive Growth and Avatar of Evolution add Adaptations and convert some physical damage taken to elemental, which your resistances then reduce. Druidic Champion is only taken to reach Furious Wellspring.

With [[Fury of the King]], the build becomes the "demon" version: its granted Molten Crash leaps to a spot and spawns fissures that [[Ferocious Roar]] (with Ancestral Cry) and [[Shockwave Totem]] detonate.

## Weapon Sets

Every skill belongs to one set. Weapon set 1 holds The Whispering Ice for its Exposure on hit, with [[Elemental Weakness]] and [[Thunderstorm]] (both at least level 18 by the endgame). Weapon set 2 is the bear talisman with all your damage skills, to get the most out of Rigwald's Ferocity.

## Leveling

Take [[Furious Slam]] at level 2 and alternate two [[Maul]] with a slam. Use [[Pounce]] to engage and to mark bosses, and [[Herald of Ash]] for Acts 1 and 2. Turning of the Seasons is a decent first ascendancy for damage if you accept the gold to respec it later. Furious Wellspring comes at level 40, Walking Calamity at 52. Aim for 150% mana regeneration, mostly from jewellery. The source guide links its Shapeshifting Druid leveling guide; save gold for the respec after the campaign.

## Playstyle

**The bear stands up.** A Furious Slam charges much more slowly if the bear is not standing. Weave a quick Maul (with Rapid Attacks) before slamming: "slap and slam". This makes all the difference.

**Mapping.** Proc Bhatair's Vengeance with a Maul when entering a map and keep it up. Keep Walking Calamity active and run; slap and slam rares. In the demon version, leap everywhere with Molten Crash and dodge roll out of its end animation, then use Walking Calamity and Ancestral Cry whenever their Glory is full. Sprint between packs.

**Bossing.** Place Thunderstorm and Elemental Weakness where the boss spawns and start Walking Calamity (and Shockwave Totems) as late as possible. Spam Maul at the start for Rage, then slap and slam. In the demon version, slam for Endurance Charges before pressing Ancestral Cry: consuming them can stretch it from about 20 to 45 seconds. Sine Aequo makes stuns and freezes much easier.

## Endgame Progression

- **Bhatair's Vengeance** is one of the biggest upgrades: a large cold damage buff that needs you to freeze. Support Maul with the planner's freeze supports and add cold damage to the weapon. If you cannot freeze, put it on Lunar Assault as a quick fix.
- **Berserk** increases Rage effect but drains 6-7% of life per second: you need life regeneration from the tree, and it fits the main tree from about level 75. You should only lose a little life while moving.
- **Charge Regulation** adds armour and energy shield for free, since the bear grants Endurance Charges when spending Rage.
- **Fury of the King** is a very high damage talisman that enables the demon version. Upgrade Shockwave Totem often: its life and resistances scale with level.
- **Shavronne's Satchel** needs a life flask with "Instant Recovery. 50% less Life Recovered"; with Berserk draining you while moving, the flask keeps firing and keeps energy shield recovering.
- **Maximum Rage** scales Vengeful Fury, Ignore Pain, Mystical Rage and Bestial Rage, and Berserk amplifies them again. Raise it with the tree (+22), the Shaman ascendancy (+13) and Tzamoto's Soul Core of Ferocity in the helmet (+4).

## Gear Priorities

Talisman damage first (Fury of the King for the demon version), cold damage for freezing, and mana regeneration on jewellery. Life, resistances and capped chaos resistance for the hardest content.

## FAQ

**Why do I struggle in Abyss?** It is some of the hardest content for melee builds. Cap chaos resistance at 75% and set up the flask for Shavronne's Satchel so you can outheal Abyss rares.

**Why does my energy shield stop recovering?** Either you are not losing life while moving with Berserk (a life flask cannot be used at full life), or your flask lacks "Instant Recovery. 50% less Life Recovered", so it stops at full life and the energy shield recovery stops with it.

**Why is my damage low?** Most likely your weapon is weak.
