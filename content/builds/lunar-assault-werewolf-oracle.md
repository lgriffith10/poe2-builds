## How It Works

Shapeshift into a Werewolf with a talisman. [[Lunar Assault]] attacks in an arc with very high freeze buildup and clears packs, and [[Herald of Ice]] shatters what freezes. On frozen enemies, [[Shred]] rips out ice fragments that fall to the ground and explode a few seconds later; [[Cross Slash]] hits hard and drags all those fragments together.

[[Pounce]] closes the gap and applies Predator's Mark: marked enemies that die summon a wolf companion (up to 7), and a marked target takes more damage for each wolf around it.

As an Oracle, The Unseen Path unlocks passives no other class can take, and Entwined Realities makes travelling across the tree cheaper.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** [[Pounce]] in and use [[Lunar Assault]]; [[Herald of Ice]] does the rest.

**Bossing.** [[Pounce]] to mark the boss, freeze it with [[Lunar Assault]], use Arctic Howl to empower your attacks, [[Shred]] during the freeze, then [[Cross Slash]] just before the boss unfreezes. Bosses are harder to freeze again right after, so use Shred alone for a few seconds. [[Savage Fury]] only needs to be active on weapon set 2 to save Spirit.

## Gear Priorities

The talisman wants physical damage, attack speed, crit and +level of attack skills. The finished setup uses Sacrosanctum, Sine Aequo and Headhunter; during the campaign, start with Entangle until you have Werewolf abilities.
