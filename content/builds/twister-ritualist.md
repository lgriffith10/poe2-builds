## How It Works

This page is drawn from the poe.ninja ladder rather than a written guide: 235 Ritualist characters use [[Twister]], and the reference character is level 99. Twister sends tornadoes through packs; players link it with **Projectile Acceleration III (97%) and Elemental Armament II (85%)**, then Rakiata's Flow (47%), Execute III (37%) and Garukhan's Resolve (30%). [[Whirling Slash]] (90%) and [[Barrage]] (83%) feed it, and [[Herald of Ice]] (45%) and [[Herald of Thunder]] (41%) add clear.

[[The Taming]] (71%) boosts damage for each elemental ailment on the enemy and lets wind skills use several elemental ground surfaces at once. [[Sacred Flame]] (65%) on weapon set 2 adds fire damage for you and your allies.

The Ritualist ascendancy is about jewellery and charms:
- **Unfurled Finger (100%)** gives you a third ring slot.
- **Mystic Attunement (99%)** gives 25% increased bonuses from your rings and amulet.
- **Intricate Sigils (64%)** adds a charm slot and 20% more charm charges.
- **Corrupted Lifeforce (49%)** grants [[Blood Boil]] and 15% more damage against enemies it affects.
- Dance with Death (74%) is the common keystone.

## Leveling

No ladder character shows a campaign, so the leveling steps on this page come from Maxroll's Spear Huntress leveling guide, a spear route with Spear Stab, Explosive Spear and Whirling Slash. The tree and ascendancy above are endgame choices: follow the leveling guide's tree during the campaign.

Switch to Twister once you have it with Projectile Acceleration III and Elemental Armament II. Take Unfurled Finger first (a third ring is a big upgrade), then Mystic Attunement, Intricate Sigils and Corrupted Lifeforce.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Throw Twister into packs and use Whirling Slash to give it more to work with; the tornadoes travel through the pack on their own and the heralds finish the rest.

**Bossing.** Cast Blood Boil on the boss, use [[Berserk]] and Barrage, then Twister and Whirling Slash close to it so several tornadoes hit at once. [[Ghost Dance]] and dodge rolls keep you safe.

## Endgame Progression

**First maps.**
- A spear with elemental damage and + to projectile skill levels, and three rings with added elemental damage to attacks and resistances.
- Lavianga's Spirits (71%) is the common flask, and cheap.
- Evasion gear with capped resistances.

**Endgame.**
- [[The Taming]] (71%) and [[Sacred Flame]] (65%) are the main uniques. [[Morior Invictus]] (64%) is the common body armour.
- Heart of the Well (79%) and From Nothing (58%) are the common jewels, Rite of Passage (71%) and Nascent Hope (60%) the common charms.
- The reference character wears [[Andvarius]] and [[Kalandra's Touch]] on its rings, and [[Wondertrap]] boots.
- [[Mageblood]] is a luxury, worn by the reference character but not required.

## Gear Priorities

Spear damage and + to projectile skill levels, then rings and amulet (Mystic Attunement makes them stronger), evasion and capped resistances. Movement speed on boots.

## FAQ

**Why three rings?** Unfurled Finger adds a ring slot, and Mystic Attunement boosts all your rings and the amulet by 25%. Invest in jewellery first.

**Why Andvarius?** It adds item rarity at the cost of 20% to all elemental resistances, a farming choice: skip it if resistances are tight.

**What is the difference with the Abyssal Lich version?** Same skill and similar links; the Ritualist relies on jewellery and charms, the Lich on mana and energy shield.
