## How It Works

This page is drawn from the poe.ninja ladder rather than a written guide. [[Twister]] sends out tornadoes that travel through packs; nearly every player links it with **Projectile Acceleration III (97%) and Elemental Armament II (85%)**, often with Rakiata's Flow (48%).

[[Whirling Slash]] (90%) and [[Barrage]] (84%) are used alongside it by most players. Heralds are common to finish packs: [[Herald of Ice]] (42%) and [[Herald of Thunder]] (39%).

## Ascendancy and Key Passives

Unfurled Finger (100%) and Mystic Attunement (99%) are taken by everyone, then Intricate Sigils (65%) and Corrupted Lifeforce (50%). Dance with Death (73%) is the main keystone.

## Gear

The Taming (69%), Sacred Flame (64%) and Morior Invictus (65%) are the most common uniques. This character wears [[Andvarius]] and [[Wondertrap]]; [[Mageblood]] and [[Kalandra's Touch]] are endgame luxuries seen on many ladder characters.
