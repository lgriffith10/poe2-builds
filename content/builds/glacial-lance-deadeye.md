## How It Works

[[Glacial Lance]] throws an icy spear that freezes and shatters enemies across the screen. [[Mirage Archer]] and the Deadeye's [[Mirage Deadeye]] add copies that attack alongside you, [[Barrage]] multiplies your projectiles, and [[Herald of Ice]] turns every shatter into an explosion. [[Called Shots]] and [[Combat Frenzy]] keep marks and frenzy charges flowing.

## Leveling

The source guide starts with [[Twister]] and [[Whirling Slash]]: Whirling Slash creates a whirlwind that empowers Twister, but only while you stay inside it, so always cast Twister from within. Keep Whirling Slash at level 1 to save mana. In Act 1, parrying and following up with [[Fangs of Frost]] creates chilled ground for extra Twister damage. Once you have [[Barrage]] and [[Ice-Tipped Arrows]] in Act 2, the Parry combo can go. Switch to [[Glacial Lance]] after completing the first Trial of Ascendancy.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Throw [[Glacial Lance]] into packs; freezes and Herald explosions clear the rest. Use [[Barrage]] on dense groups.

**Bossing.** Drop [[War Banner]], apply your marks, then combine [[Barrage]] with Glacial Lance. Keep moving: the build is not designed to stand in front of bosses.

## Gear Priorities

Spear damage, critical strike and projectile levels first, then evasion, Deflection and life on armour.

## FAQ

**Why can't I use Barrage?** With [[Olroth's Conviction]], [[Barrage]] spends Ward, so you need Ward on a piece of gear from the Kalguuran bench, ideally gloves or boots. Aim for roughly 1.5 to 2 times Barrage's Ward cost in total Ward to keep it up in the endgame.

**Why am I not generating power or endurance charges?** Most of them come from Thaumaturgic Generator, which grants charges based on the attribute type of your skills and enables Charge Regulation. It cannot be reached on the tree, so it has to be instilled on your amulet. That is expensive, so do it on a good amulet rather than one you plan to replace soon.

**How do I get a good spear?** The Akoyan Spear, the best endgame base and very good for critical strikes, drops in level 78 zones. Pick up every one and use Orbs of Transmutation and Augmentation on them looking for good modifiers; once one has two good modifiers, an Essence and a Preserved Jawbone can bring it to four.
