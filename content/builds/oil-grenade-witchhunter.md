## How It Works

[[Oil Grenade]] deals damage on impact and, if it ignites, a small explosion: it behaves like a projectile that clears white and blue monsters, which removes the usual clunkiness of grenades. The Witchhunter's Pitiless Killer makes it easy for Oil Grenade to trigger Zealous Inquisition and [[Herald of Ash]] explosions. [[Explosive Grenade]] and [[Cluster Grenade]] handle rares and bosses, [[Flash Grenade]] breaks stance and armour, [[Rapid Shot]] builds heat (a grenade consumes 15 heat for 50% of damage as extra fire), and [[War Banner]] adds 25% more damage.

Defence comes from stacking evasion to scale Sorcery Ward, a barrier against physical, chaos and elemental hits. Evasion also powers Queen of the Forest's movement speed (capped at 75%), for a fast, tanky character.

## Leveling

Grenades from level 1. Aim [[Explosive Grenade]] ahead of you while running so it hits the monsters chasing you, use Gas Grenade in Act 2, and get Oil Grenade from an Uncut Skill Gem (level 9) in Act 3; Cluster Grenade comes with a level 13 gem. Put your first supports and your first Greater Jeweller's Orb on Explosive Grenade. Cast [[Frost Bomb]] on bosses and rares. Swap Fire Attunement for Deliberation if your crossbow has lightning or cold damage. Scavenged Plating suits armour gear; with lots of evasion and enough Dexterity, [[Wind Dancer]] instead (10% more evasion per stack, up to 3).

Upgrade the crossbow often, use Iron Rings in Act 1 and gloves with added attack damage, and save Lesser Iron Runes for +1 grenade bases like the Bombard Crossbow. Early damage comes from increased elemental, attack, grenade, projectile, crossbow or two-handed damage, + Projectile or Attack levels, a physical crossbow with high percent and added physical damage, and fire or physical damage to attacks on rings and gloves.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Spam Oil Grenade on white and blue monsters, Explosive Grenade on rares, and Flash Grenade to escape danger.

**Bossing.** Activate War Banner when Glory is ready, Rapid Shot for heat, then Explosive Grenade to consume it, Cluster Grenade while Explosive Grenade is on cooldown, and Flash Grenade to break stance and armour. Crossbow Shot fills gaps when out of mana.

## Endgame Progression

**Sorcery Ward timing.** Obsessive Rituals and Ceremonial Ablution scale with armour and evasion but halve them. With weak early gear they can hurt, so take them once you move to high evasion bases. Queen of the Forest (usable from level 51) skips the hybrid armour/evasion stage and goes straight to evasion stacking; upgrade bases first.

**Early maps.**
- The crossbow is all your offence: keep upgrading its tiers and affix combination.
- Mana: Mana gained on Kill on the crossbow or a jewel, Mana Leech on Flash Grenade, and Lavianga's Spirits. A high-quality life flask counters damage over time.
- Charge Regulation needs +15 Spirit from the amulet: a Solar Amulet with its +15 implicit, or a Decree of Acuity with a Spirit corruption. It consumes Endurance Charges, which Flash Grenade generates by breaking armour with Armour Break III. Link Armour Break II and Armour Demolisher II to Flash Grenade once specced; The Molten One's Gift rewards breaking armour.

**Endgame.**
- Swap hybrid gear for the highest evasion bases, with elemental resistances at 75% and chaos as close to 75% as possible. Deflection affixes help against dangerous mechanics.
- With Queen of the Forest, drop movement speed from other sources, including boots, for more evasion.
- Rakiata's Flow on Explosive Grenade for a huge boost. With enough damage, move the armour break setup to Oil Grenade for steadier Endurance Charges. Swap Herald of Ash for Attrition on tough bosses. A Changeling Talisman on weapon swap gives Pounce for speed.

## Gear Priorities

The crossbow first (physical and fire damage, + Attack levels, attack speed, Mana on Kill), then evasion everywhere, deflection, capped resistances, and fire or physical damage to attacks on rings and gloves.

## FAQ

**How do I make grenades detonate faster?** Take Volatile Grenades on the tree and use Short Fuse II.

**How do I survive?** Stack evasion for Sorcery Ward and Queen of the Forest, and use charms against bleeding, stuns and freeze.
