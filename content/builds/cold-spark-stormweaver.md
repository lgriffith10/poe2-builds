## How It Works

This page comes from the Path of Building that Byankuu published with the video: skills, gear and tree are those of the creator's level 93 character. The write-up is ours, based on that build and on what the video describes.

[[Spark]] fires a spread of erratic projectiles. Its supports make it a clear skill: Pierce III lets them pass through enemies, Vilenta's Propulsion makes cast speed also speed up the projectiles, Pinpoint Critical makes them crit more often and Zenith II adds damage above 90% mana.

**Why "Cold" Spark.** [[Call of the Brotherhood]] converts 100% of lightning damage to cold. Spark becomes a cold skill, so it freezes, and the whole tree is built for that:
- **Glaciation** penetrates cold resistance and adds extra cold damage.
- **Thin Ice** gives 50% more damage with hits against frozen enemies.
- **Heavy Frost** makes hits ignore the positive elemental resistances of frozen enemies.
- Inescapable Cold and Endless Blizzard build freeze faster.

**Critical hits.** [[Maligaro's Virtuosity]] fixes the critical damage bonus at 250%, and the tree takes Critical Overload, Critical Exploit and Careful Assassin. Each crit also feeds [[Cast on Critical]], which casts [[Comet]] and [[Snap]] for you.

**Stormweaver.** Strike Twice and Shaper of Storms work on shock, Constant Gale gives Arcane Surge, and Storm's Recollection makes remnants come back once. [[Mana Remnants]], [[Siphon Elements]] and [[Orb of Storms]] create those remnants.

**Defence.** **Chaos Inoculation** sets life to 1 and makes you immune to chaos damage: all your defence is energy shield. Archon of the Storm grants Elemental Archon after spending your maximum mana.

## Leveling

The creator only shows the finished character, so the campaign on this page comes from Maxroll's Arc Stormweaver guide, a lightning Sorceress route close to this one. Spark itself works from the start of the campaign.

Keep Spark as lightning while leveling: the cold notables only pay off once Call of the Brotherhood converts it. Take Chaos Inoculation only when your energy shield is high enough to carry you.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Cast Spark in the direction you are going: the projectiles spread through the corridor and freeze what they hit. Cast on Critical drops Comets and Snaps on the way.

**Bossing.** Curse the boss with [[Elemental Weakness]], throw [[Frost Bomb]] for exposure and place Sigil of Power (weapon set 2 staff) where you stand. Then cast Spark from close enough that most projectiles hit the boss.

## Endgame Progression

**Key pieces.** Call of the Brotherhood comes first: it is what makes the build cold. Maligaro's Virtuosity follows for the fixed 250% critical damage bonus.

**Weapons.** A wand and a focus with + lightning spell levels, + spell levels, critical hit chance for spells, cast speed and extra elemental damage as cold.

**Jewels.** Sapphires with spell damage, critical chance and energy shield. A Time-Lost Sapphire near the critical notables adds critical hit chance for spells.

## Gear Priorities

1. Call of the Brotherhood, then Maligaro's Virtuosity.
2. Wand and focus: + spell levels, critical hit chance for spells, cast speed.
3. Energy shield on every armour piece: life is 1 with Chaos Inoculation.
4. Capped elemental resistances; chaos resistance does not matter (Chaos Inoculation).

## FAQ

**Why is my Spark still lightning?** Only Call of the Brotherhood converts it. Without the ring, the freeze notables do little.

**Why do I die at "1 life"?** Chaos Inoculation sets life to 1. Energy shield is your only buffer: raise it before taking the keystone.

**Where do Comet and Snap come from?** Cast on Critical casts them when your spells crit. They need no button.
