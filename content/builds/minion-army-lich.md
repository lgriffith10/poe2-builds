## How It Works

You are a minion build from the first zone. [[Unearth]] raises bone constructs from corpses, [[Skeletal Sniper]] provides a command skill that blasts an area, and [[Volcano]] or [[Elemental Weakness]] summon Raging Spirits during the early acts.

From the end of Act 3, Vaal Guard spectres take over most of the damage. If their burning ground and explosions are too much, the source guide offers an alternative path built around [[Skeletal Sniper]]. Around level 92, Gargantuan Wasps replace the Vaal Guards.

The permanent army: [[Skeletal Warrior]] and [[Skeletal Brute]] in front, [[Skeletal Storm Mage]] for lightning damage, [[Skeletal Cleric]] to revive, plus [[Wolf Pack]]. [[Pain Offering]] buffs them, [[Despair]] weakens bosses, and [[Elemental Weakness]] and [[Frost Bomb]] help during the campaign.

## Playstyle

**Clear.** Advance with the army and cast [[Unearth]] on corpses for extra bodies.

**Single target.** [[Pain Offering]] for rares and bosses, [[Despair]] on the boss, and [[Refutation]] to block a big hit.

## Defences

Stack energy shield, energy shield recovery and block. Soulless Form and Eternal Life work with Heavy Buffer to reduce the damage you take while you have energy shield. [[Convalescence]] adds recovery in long fights.

## Endgame Progression

**Early maps.**
- Cap resistances and meet attribute requirements first, with + minion levels on sceptre, helmet and amulet, and increased Spirit or + Spirit on body armour, amulet and sceptre to summon more minions.
- A Rattling Sceptre is best, but any Shrine Sceptre works: it gives resistances and runs Upwelling II, Strong Hearted, Cool Headed and Vitality II without extra Spirit. Put a Primate Talisman in it.
- Energy shield on every armour piece and the amulet, the helmet first. Energy shield recharge rate and faster start of recharge are good defensive modifiers.
- Runeforge Enfolding Dawn. Bones of Ullr for more minions (reservation efficiency first; the Verisium Anvil adds 5% movement speed).
- Instill Essence Infusion on the amulet. Talisman of Ralakesh in the helmet (Greater Iron Rune in a second socket). Fluxes balance resistances; once capped, Greater Iron Runes add energy shield and a Soul Core of Tacati helps chaos resistance.
- With Refutation, Runeforge one or two armour pieces for Ward, picking the base that loses the least energy shield.

**Mid.** A Sacramental or Vile Robe, Warlord Leader on the amulet, an Amanamu's Gaze in the boots at 360+ Spirit, and Idol of Egrin or Greater Iron Rune in a second helmet socket. Use Prism Guardian or a high energy shield focus with +2 minion levels (take Warding Fetish with a focus). One Evergrasping Ring for extra minion chaos damage if the rest of your gear covers resistances and attributes; Ventor's Gamble (Spirit) or a rare minion ring with resistances also work.

**Late (wasps).** A belt with "Minions' Strikes have Melee Splash" is essential: it frees the Minion Splash II slot on wasps and wolves for another damage support. Cadigan's Epiphany in the gloves and a Craiceann's Rune of Warding in the body armour.

**Min-max.** Instill Zarokh's Gift on the amulet, and The Soul Meridian or Blood of Rage on the helmet with a Raven-Touched Shard, depending on your Megalomaniac. Replace the Evergrasping Ring with rings that add minion critical chance and critical damage bonus. Mageblood (Quicksilver, Gold, Bismuth) can replace the +2 minion belt: swap Amanamu's Gaze for a Farrul's Rune of the Chase to get Quicksilver's movement speed, and put Minion Splash II back on Wolf Pack and the spectres instead of Rapid Attacks II.

## Gear Priorities

+Level of all Minion Skills and spirit on the sceptre, helmet and amulet. [[Prism Guardian]] is the shield of choice, with [[Bones of Ullr]] boots and an [[Evergrasping Ring]]. [[Enfolding Dawn]] is the source guide's campaign body armour.

## FAQ

**Which Vaal Guard should I bind as a spectre?** In Utzaal at the end of Act 3, three different monsters are all called Vaal Guard. Use [[Bind Spectre]] on the one that throws bombs (50 Spirit), not the melee one (50 Spirit) or the ranged one (60 Spirit). Their explosions can hurt performance; if they do, stay on [[Skeletal Arsonist]] or [[Skeletal Sniper]] with the same supports.

**How do I fix mana problems in the campaign?** Put [[Arcane Surge]] on [[Volcano]] and [[Efficiency I]] on [[Elemental Weakness]], and optionally another [[Efficiency I]] on [[Pain Offering]]. Once your second ascendancy gives you Soulless Form, mana should stop being an issue.

**I die too often, what should I check?** Cap your elemental resistances at 75%, aim for roughly 60% chaos resistance, and wear bases that give as much energy shield as possible.

**How do I get more support sockets for my Skeletal Warriors?** When they come from a Rattling Sceptre, their socket count follows the skill level on the sceptre: level 20 [[Skeletal Warrior]] allows five supports. A Perfect Jeweller's Orb on the sceptre adds sockets, but it does not work on corrupted or sanctified items.

**How many of each minion should I summon?** It depends on your Spirit. Keep the two free [[Skeletal Warrior]]s from the sceptre (do not summon more), one or two [[Skeletal Cleric]]s, one [[Skeletal Storm Mage]] and [[Wolf Pack]] (found in the Druid tab of an Uncut Spirit Gem), then spend all remaining Spirit on your main spectres. The late setup adds one [[Skeletal Brute]].

**What do I need before switching to Gargantuan Wasps?** Be level 92 or higher and wear a rare belt with "Minions' Strikes have Melee Splash". You do not need a new gem: the NPC can disenchant your Vaal Guard spectre gem into a clean [[Bind Spectre]] that keeps its level and sockets.
