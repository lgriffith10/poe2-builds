## How It Works

[[Lightning Spear]]'s projectiles burst into secondary projectiles on impact, which send lightning arcs to monsters in a large area; Fork makes up for not spending charges on clear. On bosses, throw [[Storm Lance]] up to its limit of 3 (4 with quality) and detonate them with [[Explosive Spear]] while the lances stay active; extra lances replace the oldest ones. [[Sniper's Mark]] adds damage and gives a Frenzy Charge to spend on Storm Lance, and [[Frost Bomb]] applies Exposure.

The Amazon's Critical Strike and Penetrate scale critical hits and add accuracy-based damage, so accuracy on the spear matters a lot. Defences stack evasion: [[Wind Dancer]] adds more, Spectral Ward turns body armour evasion into energy shield, [[Ghost Dance]] recovers it, and [[Refutation]] (from Runeforged gloves) blocks all damage for a moment.

## Leveling

Throw Explosive Spear at tough enemies and use Contagion and Frost Bomb for clear until level 5, when Lightning Spear takes over. Parry then Disengage generates Frenzy Charges for Explosive Spear. At level 15, add Sniper's Mark (with Mark of Siphoning for mana) and Storm Lance. In the Interludes, +20 Spirit from gear (120 total) runs [[Mirage Archer]] on Lightning Spear: dodge roll to create it. The ascendancy order is Predatory Instinct and In for the Kill first, then In for the Kill is swapped for Critical Strike and Penetrate.

Upgrade the spear often: higher bases have more base damage. Socket elemental runes, and save Lesser Iron Runes for high physical damage spears. A Seaglass Spear at level 51 is your best until the Akoyan Spear. Iron Rings help in Act 1, and gloves with added attack damage. Use your Verisium on the gloves at the Runeforging station to unlock Refutation.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Throw Lightning Spear; Mirage Archer and Herald of Thunder help clear. Use Storm Lance and Explosive Spear on tougher enemies.

**Bossing.** Frost Bomb, Sniper's Mark, then Storm Lances up to the cap detonated by Explosive Spear. Block boss abilities with Parry into Disengage for Frenzy Charges.

## Endgame Progression

**Early maps.**
- Affordable uniques: Tangletongue (cheap, usable early, its critical strikes roll twice and double the critical damage bonus when both succeed), Levinstone and Lycosidae. With Lycosidae, take Dance with Death, put Explosive Spear and Storm Lance on weapon set 2 with it, and keep Lightning Spear on set 1 with an empty off-hand for attack speed.
- Push critical chance as high as possible. A Tangletongue with +7% critical hit rating, a Greater Vision Rune for Penetrate and two sockets (corrupted) is a strong step.
- As much evasion and deflection as possible with capped resistances. Added damage to attacks on gloves and rings (a large share of your damage), and at least one physical damage leeched as mana.

**Endgame.**
- A rare Akoyan Spear with high accuracy (at least a tier 3 roll), critical chance and skill levels is best in slot, but expensive.
- Off-hand: Guiding Palm of the Mind for damage, Spirit and Purity of Ice; otherwise Guiding Palm of the Eye or of the Heart, or a Shrine or Omen Sceptre, with a Rabbit Idol for Spirit. The Sacred Flame's added fire damage makes Elemental Conflux effective about two thirds of the time.
- Beacon of Azis rewards high crit with damage and Spirit, a cheap option until a rare amulet with + Projectile levels. Strength on the belt for Eternal Rage (keep that gem at a low level to lower its Strength requirement), or swap Eternal Rage for Ghost Dance and Elemental Conflux for more energy shield recovery.
- Oisín's Oath on Herald of Thunder for mana while mapping, and spare Spirit on Precision II, Clarity I and II, Vitality II and Cool Headed.
- Lineage supports: Garukhan's Resolve and Rakiata's Flow on Lightning Spear for clear or Storm Lance for single target, and Rigwald's Ferocity on Lightning Spear set to weapon set 2.
- Jewels: critical damage bonus (also with spears), critical chance, and elemental, lightning or projectile damage.

## Gear Priorities

An accurate, high critical chance spear with + Attack levels, added lightning or physical damage to attacks on the spear, gloves and rings, then evasion, deflection and resistances. Movement speed on boots.

## FAQ

**How do I survive?** Stack evasion on every slot. Wind Dancer adds evasion, Spectral Ward gives 1 energy shield per 12 evasion on the body armour, Ghost Dance recovers it, and Refutation blocks all incoming damage for a short duration.

**How do I fix mana?** One physical attack damage leeched as mana on the gloves, weapon or a ring, and Oisín's Oath on Herald of Thunder to leech mana from its elemental damage.

**Why no frenzy charge gear?** The build favours raw power over charge generation and uses Fork on Lightning Spear for clear instead.
