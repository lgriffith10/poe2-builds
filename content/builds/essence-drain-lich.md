## How It Works

[[Essence Drain]] fires a projectile that applies a chaos damage-over-time debuff. [[Contagion]] marks enemies so that when they die, the effect spreads to everything nearby: one cast of each can wipe a dense pack in a cascade. Chained [[Essence Drain]] handles small groups on its own.

The Lich ascendancy carries the defences. Eldritch Empowerment grants more damage when you sacrifice energy shield; Soulless Form boosts mana regeneration and makes part of the damage you take bypass energy shield; Eternal Life then turns that bypassing damage into damage reduction while you still have energy shield. Crystalline Phylactery doubles the effect of a socketed jewel; any jewel with % maximum energy shield works.

## Playstyle

**Clear.** Cast [[Contagion]], then [[Essence Drain]] on the same pack. Pull nearby packs together before casting so the spread reaches more enemies.

**Single target.**

1. Cast [[Essence Drain]] and [[Contagion]] on the boss.
2. Stack Wither with a few casts of [[Chaos Bolt]].
3. From mid-campaign onward, place [[Sigil of Power]] before attacking; later add [[Despair]] and a [[Dark Effigy]].
4. [[Zenith II]] rewards casting at high mana: let your mana recover (or use a mana flask) between bursts.

## Defences

Stack energy shield on every armour slot and take the faster energy shield recharge nodes on the tree so Eldritch Empowerment never leaves you exposed. [[Convalescence]] adds an extra defensive layer for bosses.

## Gear Priorities

+Level of all Chaos Spell Skills on the wand and +Level of all Spell Skills on the focus and amulet are the main damage upgrades, along with cast speed. Everything else is energy shield and resistances. [[Atziri's Disdain]] is the source guide's helmet of choice.
