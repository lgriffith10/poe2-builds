## How It Works

The same core as the [Ice Shot Deadeye](/builds/ice-shot-deadeye), on the Huntress. [[Ice Shot]] bursts into shards on impact, [[Tornado Shot]] tornadoes split those projectiles again, and frozen bosses take a timed [[Snipe]]. Amazon's Critical Strike, Penetrate, Predatory Instinct and Stalking Panther bring critical strike and elemental penetration instead of the Deadeye's projectile bonuses.

## Leveling

Start with [[Frost Bomb]], [[Escape Shot]] to freeze and reposition, and [[Lightning Arrow]] to finish packs. [[Stormcaller Arrow]] handles single target until [[Snipe]]. At level 14, combine [[Freezing Salvo]] with Escape Shot for boss freezes, and use [[Ice-Tipped Arrows]] on cooldown. Add [[Ice Shot]] at level 31.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Fire [[Ice Shot]], keep [[Barrage]] and [[Ice-Tipped Arrows]] on cooldown, and use [[Tornado Shot]] on rares.

**Bossing.** Place a [[Tornado Shot]] before the boss activates, shoot [[Ice Shot]] through it, apply [[Freezing Mark]], then [[Barrage]] and [[Snipe]] once the boss is frozen.

## Gear Priorities

Bow damage and critical strike, then evasion and life. The source guide uses [[Cadiro's Gambit]] and [[The Black Insignia]].
