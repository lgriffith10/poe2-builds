## How It Works

[[Oil Barrage]] consumes Power Charges to fire chaining, forking nova projectiles across the screen. To sustain the charges, [[Devour]] the bells from [[Hollow Focus]] or the corpses of slain enemies; later, Thaumaturgic Generator, Way of the Stonefist gloves with Power Charge on crit and Ailith's Chimes on Hollow Focus automate it. Hollow Focus Technique is mandatory: you Devour a bell to start each map. [[Hand of Chayula]] applies Freezing Mark and Elemental Weakness, and [[Frost Bomb]] applies Exposure.

Defences are a large energy shield pool layered with evasion and deflection, and killing monsters off-screen before they reach you. No unique is required; Forgotten Warden is a common pick to shore up defences.

## Leveling

Level with the strongest Monk skills and swap to Oil Barrage in early maps. Rush Hollow Palm Technique by level 13 so you do not need a quarterstaff.
- **Act 1.** [[Frozen Locus]] from level 2, detonated from a distance by the large final spike of [[Glacial Cascade]].
- **Act 2.** [[Storm Wave]] detonates Frozen Locus for clear. On bosses, build freeze with Frozen Locus and Storm Wave, freeze with [[Wave of Frost]], then consume the freeze with Glacial Cascade's spike: 250% or 425% more damage.
- [[Herald of Ice]] at level 10 and [[Herald of Thunder]] in Act 3. [[Pounce]] (from a Changeling Talisman in Act 1) gives mobility and Predator's Mark.
- Save the guaranteed Greater Jeweller's Orb from Act 4 for Oil Barrage.
- Gear: + Melee levels on gloves and amulet, flat elemental damage to attacks on gloves and rings, life and resistances, 20-30% movement speed boots. Horns of Bynden and Luminous Pace in trade leagues.

## Playstyle

**Mapping.** Devour a bell at the start of the map, then clear with Oil Barrage, Devouring bells and corpses as needed. If playing aggressively is hard, avoid rolling into packs and let the projectiles chain and fork from a distance.

**Bossing.** Hand of Chayula and Frost Bomb, then channel Oil Barrage. Avoid [[Refutation]] while clearing to prevent heavy stuns.

## Endgame Progression

**Swapping to Oil Barrage (early maps).** Basic upgrades cost 1 to 2 Exalted Orbs per slot. Get a 300+ elemental DPS Spiny Talisman (or one with similar critical chance) for 5 to 20 Exalted Orbs, plus 10 to 20 for augments or a Perfect Essence of Battle. To craft it, use Transmutation and Augmentation on normal bases, then a Regal Orb once you hit an elemental damage modifier. Uncut Skill Gems (level 14) and Uncut Support Gems (level 5) drop more often in maps.
- Use Nova Projectiles I on Oil Barrage: Nova Projectiles II stops you from socketing all your gems.
- Until Thaumaturgic Generator or Power Charge gloves, Blasphemy with Repulsion, Living Lightning II, Culmination II and Ailith's Chimes generates more charges, at the cost of Charge Regulation and Ghost Dance.
- Gloves after Way of the Stonefist, in this order: critical damage bonus (becomes base critical chance), attack speed (becomes Onslaught on hit), flat damage (becomes damage as extra), percent defences (become more global evasion and energy shield). Arcane Surge on crit (becomes Power Charge on crit) comes in the endgame. Runeforge the Fists of Stone at the Verisium Anvil to cast Refutation.

**Level 77+.** Upgrades of 5 to 10 Exalted Orbs per slot, a talisman with + Melee or Attack levels and 380+ elemental DPS (10 to 50 Exalted Orbs, or 1 to 2 Divine Orbs), level 17-18 uncut gems, and the third and fourth ascendancies. **Instill Thaumaturgic Generator on the amulet** for charge generation (again whenever you change amulets). A Forgotten Warden for survivability: link Oil Barrage in its Spirit Vessel for single target, plus Romira's Requital and, last, Amanamu's Tithe. Time-Lost jewels with critical chance and damage. At 50%+ critical chance, swap Pinpoint Critical for Elemental Armament II.

**Late maps to endgame.** A 490+ elemental DPS talisman with 15%+ critical chance and + levels, 150% increased item rarity, 35%+ movement speed boots with evasion and deflection, a better Forgotten Warden, 3-4 mod Time-Lost jewels, close to 100% critical chance on Oil Barrage, and level 19-20 gems at 20% quality.

**Aspirational.** The best talisman you can afford with attack speed (many listings show inflated DPS from Greater Iron Runes), Thaumaturgic Generator on the helmet with a Raven-Touched Shard and Zarokh's Gift on the amulet, five-mod jewels at 20% quality, and level 21 gems. With Rakiata's Flow, use a level 21, 0% quality Oil Barrage and remove Elemental Weakness from Hand of Chayula. Rite of Passage spirits by priority: Stag, Cat, Wolf, then Owl and Bear.

## Gear Priorities

Talisman elemental DPS, critical chance and + levels, Way of the Stonefist gloves, energy shield, evasion and deflection on armour, and increased item rarity once damage allows.

## FAQ

**How do I survive better?** Upgrade item bases for more energy shield or evasion (body armour, helmet and boots first), take more energy shield, evasion or deflection on the tree, cap elemental resistances, and wear the right charms: resistance charms, Amethyst for chaos, Stone for stuns, Thawing for freeze, Dousing for ignite and Staunching for bleed.

**How much item rarity should I aim for?** Increased item rarity also raises the chance for currency to upgrade to rarer currency. Aim for 100% in early maps and 150% as soon as possible; beyond that, returns diminish.
